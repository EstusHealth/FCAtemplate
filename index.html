0 · Referral, Scope & Stream
The purpose drives the emphasis of the report. SOP §5.1: the reason for referral is also what justifies the assessment hours billed for an FCA rather than ongoing therapy — so it has to be specific.
0.1 · Consent & the Transparency Conversation
SOP §2.0 — Client transparency. Explain at the outset why the report will be deficit-focused: it is a requirement for securing funding, not a judgement of the participant's worth or ability. Do this before the interview starts, not after the family reads the draft.


0.2 · Documents to Obtain
Request these before the assessment. Anything still outstanding at report time has to be named as a limitation in Stage 5 — the builder will do that for you, but a report that lists five missing documents is a weaker report.
0.3 · Assessment Kit & Set-Up
Laid out before the participant arrives. Mark anything genuinely not required as N/A and say why — a blank is not an answer, because the checklist prints as a record of what was prepared.
0.4 · Field Record & Additional Documents
A report can only be as good as the record it is written from. Log observations as they happen — in the interview, in the home, during testing. Every impairment you assert later must point back to a note logged here. The Field notes button stays available on every stage.
Whatever you select prints in the report's methodology statement. AI may refine or restructure wording drawn from your own record. It cannot supply observations, functional findings or clinical reasoning that were not recorded at the time — and this builder will not export a report with more auto-drafted text than field record behind it.

3 · Interview Run Sheet
Narrative fields accept tokens: {name} {first} {He} {he} {His} {his} {him} {is} {was} {has} — they adapt to the participant's name and pronouns. Open the prompts on each domain and ask them. Domains marked core must be completed or marked not applicable with a reason.
4 · Background & Context
Constitution Directive 2.4 — each of these four sections follows the Golden Thread: state the fact, then immediately name its functional consequence and the evidence for it.
